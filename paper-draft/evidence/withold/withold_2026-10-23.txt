PREDICTION -- the witness holds a blamed server until it serves trials
Registered 2026-10-23, BEFORE any wh_* run.

What is being tested. When the witness acted (witact_2026-10-23_OUTCOME.md)
the remaining loss was a cycle: a blamed server's verdict lapsed once its
failures left the witness's 60 s window, the gate released it, and it failed
~10 more connections before being blamed again. With
controller.witness.act.hold (controller/switch_witness.py, WitnessHold) a
blamed server is HELD: the gate signal stays on after the verdict lapses, the
server gets one trial connection every 10 s (installed above the quarantine
drop rules, like a probation trial), and it is released only after the
WITNESS sees 3 trials in a row served -- switch counters show a full
response and the client reports a success. The controller's own records do
not judge the trial; with forged evidence they would pass it. On release the
witness forgets the server's old failures.

Arms (config/params_wh_*.yaml; pinned by tests/test_witness_hold_arms.py):
  wh_control_hold  witfix_control + act + hold         (false holds, cost)
  wh_forge_act     lv_forge_evidence + act             (fresh twin of wa_forge_act)
  wh_forge_hold    lv_forge_evidence + act + hold
  wh_drop_act      witfix_drop + act                   (srv6 blackhole from 30 s,
  wh_drop_hold     witfix_drop + act + hold             srv1 50% grayhole from 40 s)

Basis.
  * Tests (tests/test_witness_hold.py): a held server gets exactly one trial
    per interval and no other traffic; trials ignore Ā and trust but respect
    admission; only connections routed AS trials count; a failure resets the
    run; release after 3 in a row forgets the server's old failures. Breaking
    any of: the forgetting, the trial-only counting, or the reset, fails a test.
  * wa_forge_act (acting, no hold): srv3 got 43-51 connections between onset
    and its first quarantine, 148 [139, 157] in all after onset; quarantined
    84.3%; honest task success 97.54% [97.45, 97.64].
  * witfix_drop (gate only, witness observing): srv6 46 [34, 59] connections
    after onset, quarantined 49%; srv1 97 [56, 137], quarantined 56%; honest
    task success 98.57% [98.35, 98.79].
  * Arithmetic: a held blackhole costs ~1 trial connection per 10 s (~24 over
    a 240 s attack) instead of the act-only cycle. That clearly beats the
    forge case's ~100 post-detection connections, but is about what the gate
    already allows a plain blackhole, so for the drop arms the sign is not
    predicted. A 50% grayhole passes 3 trials in a row after ~14 trials on
    average (2 + 4 + 8), i.e. ~140 s held: the consecutive-pass rule's known
    weakness, stated before the runs.

  H1  wh_control_hold: no honest server held in >= 4/5 runs; honest task
      success >= 99.9% in every run.
  H2  wh_forge_hold: srv3 held 5/5 and released in <= 1/5 runs; quarantined
      >= 95% of the post-onset time; <= 100 connections after onset; honest
      task success >= 98.5%, interval disjoint above wh_forge_act's.
  H3  wh_forge_act reproduces wa_forge_act: honest task success within 1
      point of 97.5%.
  H4  wh_drop_hold: srv6 held 5/5 and never released; its connections after
      onset within 20 of wh_drop_act's (the hold neither helps nor hurts much
      against a blackhole the gate already contains).
  H5  wh_drop_hold: srv1 (50% grayhole) released at least once in >= 3/5
      runs.
  H6  Across all 25 runs: no honest party blamed by the witness and no honest
      server held.

Not predicted, reported as measured: honest task success in the drop arms;
how long srv1 is held per hold.
