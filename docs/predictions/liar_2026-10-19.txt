WHAT THE MODEL SAYS
1. A consistent lie (claim 0, busy counter faked to match) never pays: below
   fair share at every occupancy from 0.01 to 0.50. The honesty term
   penalises the gap between claimed and expected busy time on every task,
   and p2c decides each comparison by score, so even a 0.002 trust deficit
   loses the comparison. It is caught by the gate from occupancy 0.30 up
   (once share x N x occupancy - claim > 0.40).
2. The SPLIT CLAIM (instantaneous cpu_load = 0, busy counter truthful) is the
   hole: routing reads the raw cpu_load, the honesty check reads the busy
   counter. It gains at every occupancy >= 0.10 (+1.5 points at 0.10, +5.2 at
   0.40, +8.3 at 0.50) and is never caught by the gate at occupancy <= 0.40.
3. The stealthy liar (adaptive, consistent) is dominated by the split claim
   and stays below fair share.
4. No liar exceeds the p2c+eps ceiling of 24.4 %.

Known ways the live system differs from the model, each of which could move
the result: UCB1 changes the selection weights during a run (its arm
[0.34, 0.50, 0.16] gives the cpu claim the most weight, which favours the
split claim); live claims are polled once per second; live latency includes
flow installation; live probation has a stale-evidence rule.
